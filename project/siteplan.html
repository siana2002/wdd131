Tongan Culture

Site Name

Tongan Culture

This name was chosen because it clearly represents a website dedicated to sharing the traditions, history, language, and customs of Tonga.

Site Purpose

This website provides information about Tongan culture, including its traditions, history, language, food, celebrations, and values. It is designed to educate visitors and promote appreciation of Tonga's rich cultural heritage.

Scenarios

  • What are the most important traditions and customs in Tongan culture?
  • What foods are traditionally eaten in Tonga?

Color Scheme

  • Red (#C8102E): Used for headings, buttons, and important accents throughout the website.
  • Black (#000000): Used for body text, the navigation bar, and the footer.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Used as the main background color to keep the website clean and easy to read.

Typography

  • Roboto: Used for the entire website, including headings, body text, navigation, and other content. Roboto was chosen because it is clean, modern, and highly readable on both desktop and mobile devices.
